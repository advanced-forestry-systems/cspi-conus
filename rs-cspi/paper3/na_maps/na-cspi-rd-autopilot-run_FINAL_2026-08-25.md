## Headline

Two of the five items changed the state of the project, not just its paperwork. ESI30, reported closed on 19 August, is reopened: the closing gate test measured the wrong quantity, and a corrected rerun clears both the registered gate and the project's own admission bar by a wide margin. Job 13728908, believed running as of 19 August in every workspace document including this morning's own review, had actually failed that same day; a rebuilt RD30 assembly pipeline surfaced two further latent defects nobody had seen because no prior run had gotten far enough to hit them, then correctly caught a genuine 9.9 percent map-versus-plot discrepancy at a gate that did not exist before today. Neither RD30 product is promoted. The other three items closed cleanly: six files no longer describe an experimental raster as the CSPI v2 of record, a Zenodo correction draft no longer risks publishing a number that contradicts the file it ships next to, and an interior-nodata probe the project had flagged as missing for six days now exists and found two real defects, both outside the flagship products it was run to protect.

## Track by track

**Track A, six banner edits (component-count reconciliation).** Applied verbatim per `cspi-component-count-reconciliation_FINAL_2026-08-19.md`'s file-by-file instructions: a status banner on `CONUS_CSPI_v2_5component_definition_2026-07-28.md`, dated annotation blocks in the header comments of `mosaic_na_30m.R` and `na_cspi_build.R`, and one-line dated annotations in `REVIEW_PLAN_when_landed.md`, `20260627_bgi-cspi-conus_state_and_next_steps.md`, and `active-projects/bgi-cspi-conus/NEWS.md`. Also added a superseding annotation to `na-30m-blocker-review_FINAL_2026-08-19.md` itself, since its two headline claims about ESI30 and job 13728908 are now the exact kind of stale inherited number (F9) this project's own discipline exists to catch, and it is the single most-cited status document in the folder.

**Track B, Zenodo v1.4.0 draft description (E1).** The paste-ready description stated flatly that the corrected median relative density is 0.6970, true of the RD_uncap comparator scale the erratum's arithmetic is measured on, false of the file the run sheet actually directs be shipped (the harmonised rebuild in `conus_rd_rebuilt_uncapped.csv`, verified against `CONUS_RD_BUILD_2026-08-04.md` at a median of 0.428 uncapped, a different number because it also corrects the diameter reference and the rebuild population, not only the 2.471 units factor). Added a distinguishing sentence to the description block itself, in place, stating both numbers and which file each belongs to, with an explicit unverified tag on the shipped file's median restricted to the same 123,173-plot comparison population, to be re-measured at staging per the gate checklist's own Stage A requirement. Also conditioned the version-note sentence claiming the version "replaces the defective response and affected products," since that is true only under run sheet Option 1 and Aaron has not yet chosen between Option 1 and Option 2.

**Track C, ESI30 gate rerun.** Full report: `esi30-gate-rerun_FINAL_2026-08-25.md`. ESI_true and ESI_fuzz, confirmed by reading `nfi_extract_truecoords.R` and `nfi_predict_esi.R` on Cardinal, are outputs of the existing North America ClimateNA ranger model applied at plot coordinates, not field measurements, exactly the identity the 19 August red team flagged as a live risk. The corrected gate fits a genuine plot-to-embedding model against top height, a measured response, and reports within-block share of the model's explained variance rather than raw response variance. Result: 50.2 percent within-block explained variance against the registered 20 percent bar, blocked predictive R^2 of 0.489 and calibration slope of 1.128 against the project's own BGI30-precedent admission bar of R^2 at least 0.25 and slope at least 0.50. Both pass. ESI30 is live, pending the two items the track logged rather than resolved: no leave-one-province-out transfer test has been run against this result (BGI30, on the same admission bar, passed blocked CV and then failed transfer catastrophically, so this is not a formality), and the reversal has not yet been swept into whatever other memos still cite the 19 August "closed" verdict beyond the one file annotated in Track A today.

**Track D, RD30 assembly gates and job 13728908.** Full report: `rd30-assembly-gatefix-and-diagnosis_FINAL_2026-08-25.md`. Job 13728908 failed cleanly on its own pre-registered F5 area gate 31 minutes in (mapped area 356.7 Mha against a [100, 350] Mha window anchored to FIA CONUS forestland, about 251 Mha), not a crash, and every document calling it "running" was wrong. The root cause is a population mismatch the same red team pass had already named in a different context (finding C1): the RD-positive pixel mask and FIA's forestland definition are not the same population, so no defensible re-derivation of the F5 window exists today. F5 was demoted to informational rather than widened without justification, and A1, the map-anchored gate the red team actually asked for, was added in its place. Getting a verdict out of the rebuilt pipeline took two more infrastructure fixes nobody had reason to know about before today (a `terra` package rebuilt against a newer GDAL ABI than Cardinal's module provides, and a `system2()` shell-quoting bug in the mosaic-build step that existed since rev 1 but had never been reached by a run that got far enough to trigger it) before the pipeline finally ran end to end and the new A1 gate correctly stopped it: the assembled calfix mosaic, sampled at all 123,173 actual FIA plot locations, has a median of 0.766 against the plot RD_field median of 0.697, a 9.9 percent relative gap outside the 0.02 tolerance F9 uses elsewhere in this same pipeline. A2, C1, C2, and C3 were implemented as specified. Both RD30 CONUS products remain PROVISIONAL. The 0.069 gap is genuinely new work, not part of today's ask, and is the top item in Track D's own unknowns ledger; a working hypothesis (mean-based hex-SAE calibration against a right-skewed, median-sensitive target) is recorded but not chased down.

**Track E, interior-nodata F1 probe.** Full report: `f1-interior-nodata-probe_FINAL_2026-08-25.md`, data: `f1-interior-nodata-probe_DATA_2026-08-25.csv`. Built the point-lattice probe the 19 August F1 sweep flagged as its own stated gap, spanning the full declared extent of each raster rather than a bounding-box corner check, at a spacing chosen against Prince Edward Island, the smallest jurisdiction in play. Two real defects found. `cspi/_intermediate_canada_soils_2km.tif` is total nodata at all 93,366 sampled points across its entire declared extent, not selective masking of one province, the whole file appears never to have been populated; `cspi/Canada_FIPI_composite_2km_strict.tif` is similarly void. Both are intermediate or diagnostic files, not the flagship CSPI, BGI, ESI, or RD products. More relevant to the two tracks running in parallel today: 34 of 60 stratified-sampled tiles (57 percent) in the RD30 uncertainty tile family are at or near total nodata, reading as a partially-completed layer rather than masking; the primary RD30 data, quality-mask, and calibration tile families, the CONUS RD30 mosaic, the Canadian NFI embedding tiles, and the full cspi_v7 CONUS composite family all came back clean. A genuine new discovery along the way: the CONUS RD30 product on disk is four parallel 24,544-tile families, not the one file the 19 August sweep's 44-file inventory covered, an order of magnitude more surface area than anyone had audited before this afternoon.
